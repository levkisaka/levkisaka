# 👋 About Me

Hello! I'm **Levi Kisaka**, a **Full-Stack Software Developer** focused on building scalable, secure, and business-driven software solutions.

I build and maintain **web applications, mobile applications, APIs, multi-tenant SaaS platforms, workforce management systems, and business automation tools**. My work spans the full development lifecycle—from database design and backend architecture to frontend interfaces, mobile applications, integrations, deployment, troubleshooting, and production support.

I particularly enjoy working on complex systems where software needs to interact with **real-world business processes, mobile devices, biometric equipment, NFC, payments, messaging platforms, and large volumes of operational data**.

My approach is practical: understand the business problem, design a reliable technical solution, implement it end-to-end, and continuously improve it based on real-world usage.

## 🚀 What I Do

* **Full-Stack Application Development** — Building complete applications across the frontend, backend, database, APIs, and infrastructure.
* **Multi-Tenant SaaS Systems** — Designing tenant-aware platforms with isolated business data, configurable features, roles, permissions, authentication, and tenant-specific workflows.
* **Business & Enterprise Systems** — Developing systems for workforce management, HR, attendance, leave, shifts, patrol operations, quality control, reporting, payments, and operational management.
* **Web Applications** — Building responsive web applications using **Next.js, React, TypeScript, JavaScript, Laravel, and PHP**.
* **Backend & API Development** — Designing REST APIs, authentication systems, business logic, integrations, background processing, and scheduled workflows.
* **Mobile Applications** — Building **React Native and hybrid mobile applications**, including offline workflows, device synchronization, permissions, GPS, NFC, biometric integrations, and Android-specific functionality.
* **Reporting & Data Processing** — Working with large datasets, queued exports, scheduled reports, Excel/PDF generation, background jobs, notifications, and performance optimization.
* **Device & Hardware Integrations** — Integrating systems with **biometric devices, fingerprint verification, NFC, access/attendance devices, mobile hardware, and other field equipment**.
* **Payments & Messaging** — Integrating **M-Pesa, SMS platforms, payment workflows, email notifications, and third-party communication services**.
* **Infrastructure & Production Support** — Managing Linux-based production environments, web servers, databases, queues, deployments, troubleshooting, and system performance.
