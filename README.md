# 👋 About Me

Hello! I'm **Levi Kisaka**, a software developer focused on building **scalable, secure, and business-driven software solutions**.

I specialize in developing **multi-tenant SaaS platforms, web and mobile applications, backend APIs, integrations, and data-intensive business systems**. I enjoy solving complex engineering problems—from optimizing large-scale reporting and background processing to integrating mobile devices, biometrics, NFC, payments, and third-party APIs.

My goal is to build reliable software that doesn't just work, but **improves business workflows, automation, and operational efficiency**.

## 🚀 What I Do

* **SaaS & Multi-Tenant Systems** — Designing tenant-aware applications with configurable features, permissions, billing, usage tracking, and isolated business data.
* **Web Applications** — Building modern, responsive applications using **Next.js, React, TypeScript, and JavaScript**.
* **Backend & APIs** — Developing robust APIs and business logic using **Laravel, PHP, Node.js, and MySQL**.
* **Mobile Applications** — Building **React Native** and hybrid mobile applications with device integrations such as **NFC, biometrics, fingerprint verification, and device synchronization**.
* **HR & Workforce Systems** — Developing attendance, leave, shift management, biometric verification, patrol control, scheduling, and reporting solutions.
* **Reporting & Data Processing** — Designing reliable reporting systems with **large dataset processing, queued exports, scheduled reports, Excel/PDF generation, and background jobs**.
* **Third-Party Integrations** — Integrating **SMS, payment platforms, authentication services, external APIs, and business automation workflows**.
* **Infrastructure & Deployment** — Working with **Linux servers, Apache, Nginx, MySQL, queues, process managers, and production deployments**.
